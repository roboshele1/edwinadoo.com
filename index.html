Why I built it
Most retail investing tools display data. They don't help you decide. I wanted the same rule-based analytical rigor used by professional funds applied to my own portfolio without the emotion and noise.
How it works
Jupiter integrates live market data from multiple APIs, a Kelly Criterion capital allocation engine, and a background scanner that evaluates 12,000+ securities against real-time market conditions then surfaces decisions, not data.
What it took
Defined the problem. Structured the solution. Executed end-to-end no team, no engineering background, no playbook. From identifying the user need to shipping a modular analytics platform in production.
What it proves
The most valuable skill isn't technical fluency. It's hypothesis-driven thinking knowing what needs to be true, what data confirms it, and what's the fastest path to an answer. That skill works everywhere.
